# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **Antigravity Theme** (`@django9944/antigravity-theme`) and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy technical community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Focusing on concrete technical evidence, reproducible APCA/WCAG contrast measurements, and constructive feedback.
- Respecting project scope guardrails ([VISION.md](VISION.md)) and maintainer review bandwidth.
- Taking full human accountability for submitted changes, including AI-assisted contributions ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, credentials, or sensitive data without explicit permission.
- Spamming automated or unreviewed pull requests that disregard project guidelines.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, code comments) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at `angusyoung9944@gmail.com` (or via [Discord @angusyoung9944](https://discord.com/users/953288159876239420)). For security vulnerabilities, please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
